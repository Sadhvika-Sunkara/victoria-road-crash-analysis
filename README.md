# 🚗 Victoria Road Crash Analysis

Analysing 200,000+ Victorian road crashes (2012–2025) to identify 
when, where, and who is most at risk.

## Key facts from the data
- 200,352 crashes recorded
- 467,730 people involved
- 3,606 fatalities over 13 years
- Data source: Transport Victoria Open Data Portal
